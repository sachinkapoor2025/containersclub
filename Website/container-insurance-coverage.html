Protect Against Damage, Loss, Theft & Natural Disasters During Transit

Container shipping involves numerous risks that can result in significant financial losses without proper insurance coverage. Our policies protect against common perils such as container damage from rough handling, cargo loss due to vessel accidents, theft during land transportation, and natural disasters like hurricanes, floods, and earthquakes.
