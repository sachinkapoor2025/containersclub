Container Types & Sizes Guide for Shipping in the USA

Understanding container types and sizes is essential for efficient shipping and logistics operations across the United States. Our comprehensive guide provides detailed information about standard dry containers, high-cube units, refrigerated containers, open-top containers, flat-rack containers, and specialized options. Whether you're shipping general cargo, temperature-sensitive goods, oversized equipment, or hazardous materials, selecting the right container type and size ensures safe transport and optimal space utilization. This guide helps businesses, freight forwarders, and logistics professionals make informed decisions when planning domestic and international shipments.

Explore Standard & Specialized Container Types for Every Cargo Need

We offer a wide range of container types designed to accommodate various cargo requirements. Standard dry containers are ideal for general cargo, while high-cube containers provide additional vertical space for lightweight, bulky items. Refrigerated containers maintain precise temperature control for perishable goods, pharmaceuticals, and temperature-sensitive products. Open-top containers allow easy loading of tall or heavy machinery, and flat-rack containers are perfect for oversized cargo that cannot fit through standard container doors. Specialized containers include insulated units, tank containers for liquids, and hazardous material containers with enhanced safety features.

Find the Right Container Size — 20ft, 40ft, High Cube & More

Choosing the correct container size is crucial for maximizing cargo capacity and minimizing shipping costs. Our standard offerings include 20ft and 40ft containers, with high-cube variants providing extra height for increased volume. For specialized needs, we also provide 10ft, 45ft, and 53ft containers. Each size comes with specific internal dimensions, door openings, and weight limits that affect loading efficiency and cargo security. Understanding these specifications helps shippers optimize space utilization, reduce freight charges, and ensure compliance with transportation regulations across U.S. highways, rail networks, and ports.

Compare Container Specifications, Dimensions & Capacity Charts

Our detailed container specifications include internal and external dimensions, door measurements, tare weight, maximum payload, and cubic capacity. These specifications are essential for proper cargo planning, load distribution, and compliance with weight restrictions. We provide comprehensive capacity charts that show how different cargo types and packaging configurations affect container utilization. This information helps logistics managers calculate optimal loading patterns, prevent overloading, and ensure cargo stability during transit. Accurate specifications also facilitate smooth customs clearance and port handling operations.

Learn About Container Features — Ventilation, Insulation & Security

Modern shipping containers come equipped with various features designed to protect cargo and enhance security. Ventilation systems in dry containers prevent moisture buildup and maintain air circulation for sensitive goods. Insulated containers provide thermal protection for temperature-sensitive products without active refrigeration. Security features include tamper-proof locks, seal systems, and tracking devices to prevent theft and ensure cargo integrity. Understanding these features helps shippers select containers that provide the appropriate level of protection for their specific cargo requirements while maintaining compliance with international shipping standards.

Download Container Codes, Markings & Compliance Standards

Container identification codes, markings, and compliance standards are essential for international shipping operations. Each container has a unique identification number, size code, and type code that facilitate tracking and handling. Standard markings include maximum gross weight, tare weight, and cubic capacity information. Compliance with ISO standards, CSC (Container Safety Convention) plates, and customs regulations ensures containers meet international safety and security requirements. We provide downloadable resources containing container codes, marking specifications, and compliance guidelines to help shippers maintain regulatory compliance and operational efficiency.
